Eco Impact

Reduces microplastic water pollution

This project focuses on using loofah, a natural and biodegradable plant, as a sustainable alternative to plastic-based household products such as sponges and cleaning pads. Plastic cleaning materials are widely used but contribute significantly to environmental pollution, especially microplastics in water systems. Our solution promotes loofah as an eco-friendly substitute that is fully compostable and safe for the environment.

The project combines sustainable agriculture with product innovation by cultivating loofah using low-resource farming methods and transforming it into affordable, high-quality cleaning products. By doing so, we aim to reduce plastic waste, support local farmers, and create green economic opportunities within communities.
