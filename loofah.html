Transforming Agriculture into Tech

Microplastics from household cleaning sponges are devastating global water systems. The Loofah project solves this by replacing synthetics with a highly optimized, 100% biodegradable organic alternative.

By applying modern manufacturing psychology to sustainable agriculture, we created a product that works better than plastic, bolsters local farming economies, and leaves absolutely zero toxic trace.
